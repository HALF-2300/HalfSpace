# 🚀 HALFSPACE — Quick Start Guide

## Launch in 30 Seconds

```bash
npm run dev
```

Then open: **http://localhost:3000/space/halfspace**

## 🎮 What to Try First

### 1. Move Your Cursor Slowly
Watch the stars react and move toward your gaze.

### 2. Type "VOID"
Flip to the dark dimension. Everything inverts.

### 3. Click the Time Icon (⏳)
Open the Quantum Timeline and travel through epochs.  
Drag the slider to watch the galaxy form and collapse.

### 4. Create a Galaxy (✨)
Click bottom-left, choose a word or emotion.  
Your galaxy is born into the cosmos forever.

### 5. Stay Still
Don't move your cursor for 5 seconds.  
The AI will notice and question you.

### 6. Type "DREAM"
Describe a dream. It becomes cosmic coordinates.

### 7. Type "INFINITE"
Scroll through billions of years of cosmic history.

### 8. Open Console (F12)
See cosmic transmissions appear randomly.
