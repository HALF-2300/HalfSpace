# 🌌 HALFSPACE — A Portal Beyond Reality

> *"We are stardust, thinking about itself."*

## ✨ What is HALFSPACE?

HALFSPACE is not just a website — it's a **living, breathing digital universe** that bends reality and merges science with dreams. It's an immersive experience where the cosmos watches you, remembers you, and speaks to you.

## 🚀 Quick Start

```bash
npm run dev
```

Then navigate to: **http://localhost:3000/space/halfspace**

## 🎯 Features

### 1. 🧠 The Living Universe (AI + Sentience)

The universe is **alive**. It watches, remembers, and reacts to you.

- **Reactive Starfield**: Stars respond to your gaze, moving subtly toward where you look
- **Memory System**: The site remembers your visits and previous interactions
- **AI Consciousness**: An AI entity observes you and asks philosophical questions
- **Lingering Detection**: If you stare too long at a point, the AI notices and questions your intent

*"You've been here before."*

### 2. ⏳ Quantum Time Bending

Travel through time and witness galaxy evolution in real-time.

- **Time Slider**: Navigate from galaxy formation (-13.8B years) to heat death (+10B years)
- **Dynamic Evolution**: Watch the central galaxy form, stabilize, and eventually collapse into a black hole
- **Locked Timelines**: Some moments in time are forbidden — discover clues to unlock them
- **Era Labels**: See what cosmic era you're in (Primordial Formation → Event Horizon)

*"This time should not exist."*

### 3. 🪞 Parallel Realities

Experience mirror dimensions where truth reveals itself.

- **Dimension Flipping**: Type **"VOID"** to enter the dark dimension
- **Visual Inversion**: Colors invert, the AI's tone changes, reality warps
- **Hidden Truths**: The dark side reveals what the light hides
- **Reality Distortion Effects**: Visual waves ripple across space when dimensions shift

*"Welcome to the side that was never meant to be seen."*

### 4. 🌠 User-Generated Galaxies

Create your own galaxies from words and emotions.

- **Word Mode**: Type any word — it becomes a star system with unique color and position
- **Emotion Mode**: Choose emotions (joy, fear, wonder, etc.) that manifest as colored nebulae
- **Persistent Universe**: Your galaxies drift eternally through HalfSpace
- **Shared Cosmos**: All visitors' galaxies exist in the same universe

*"Your thoughts become stars."*

### 5. ⚛️ Entangled Visitors (Quantum Chat)

When two people visit simultaneously, they become quantumly entangled.

- **Paired Consciousness**: See ghost trails of another visitor's cursor
- **Light Pulses**: Click to send light signals to your entangled partner
- **Connection Line**: A subtle line connects your viewpoints
- **Anonymous**: No chat, only presence — pure cosmic connection

*"You are not alone in the void."*

### 6. 🔮 Dream Sync Protocol

Interpret your dreams as cosmic coordinates.

- **Dream Input**: Describe a dream in natural language
- **AI Interpretation**: Dreams are analyzed and mapped to space sectors
- **Coordinate Generation**: Each dream links to a specific region of space
- **Historical Records**: Learn about the sector your dream connects to
- **Persistent Dreams**: Your dream coordinates are saved forever

*Type "DREAM" anywhere to open*

*"You entered Sector X-97. Last recorded by an explorer 600 years ago."*

### 7. ∞ Infinite Scroll of Existence

Journey through cosmic history in an endless scroll.

- **Time Depth**: Scroll deeper to travel further back in time
- **Cosmic Fossils**: Discover artifacts and memories of ancient civilizations
- **Era Progression**: Recent History → Ancient Era → Primordial Age → Before Time
- **The End/Beginning**: Reach the ultimate boundary where time itself becomes uncertain

*Type "INFINITE" to open*

*"You are the first. Or the last."*

### 8. 🌌 Cosmic Glitches & Whispers

The universe communicates through intentional "errors."

- **Star Messages**: Stars rearrange to form words (WE SEE YOU, LISTEN, VOID)
- **Console Transmissions**: Unknown signals appear in the browser console
- **Cosmic Whispers**: Ambient audio hints play subtly
- **Random Events**: The AI whispers unpredictably
- **Glitch Text**: Reality glitches to deliver messages

*"The universe talks back."*

## 🎮 Secret Commands

Type these words anywhere on the page to unlock features:

- **VOID** — Flip to the dark dimension
- **DREAM** — Open the Dream Sync Protocol
- **INFINITE** — Open the Infinite Scroll

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture

### Built With

- **Next.js 14** — React framework
- **Three.js + React Three Fiber** — 3D universe rendering
- **Zustand** — State management (persisted to localStorage)
- **TypeScript** — Type safety
- **CSS3** — Custom animations and effects

### File Structure

```
app/space/halfspace/
├── page.tsx                    # Main entry point
├── HalfspaceExperience.tsx     # Core experience component
├── halfspace.css               # All styling
├── store/
│   └── halfspaceStore.ts       # Zustand state management
├── lib/
│   └── visitorTracking.ts      # Visitor session tracking
└── components/
    ├── LivingUniverse.tsx      # 3D starfield & galaxy
    ├── QuantumTimeline.tsx     # Time travel slider
    ├── ParallelRealities.tsx   # Dimension flipper
    ├── UserGalaxyCreator.tsx   # Galaxy creation UI
    ├── AIConsciousness.tsx     # AI watcher & messages
    ├── EntangledVisitors.tsx   # Quantum pairing
    ├── CosmicWhispers.tsx      # Glitches & transmissions
    ├── DreamSyncProtocol.tsx   # Dream interpreter
    └── InfiniteScroll.tsx      # Time scroll experience
```

### State Management

HALFSPACE uses Zustand with localStorage persistence to remember:

- Visitor ID (unique per person)
- Visit count and history
- User-created galaxies
- Dream entries and coordinates
- Quantum time position

### Performance

- **5,000 stars** in the reactive starfield
- **10,000 particles** in the evolving galaxy
- **60 FPS** target on modern hardware
- **WebGL** required (fallback graceful degradation possible)
