# 🚀 Enhanced Space Journey - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Get Started in 3 Steps!

### Step 1: Start the Development Server
```bash
npm run dev
```

### Step 2: Open Your Browser
```
http://localhost:9200/space-journey-enhanced
```

### Step 3: Launch Your Journey!
Click the **"🚀 Launch Enhanced Journey"** button and enjoy!

---

## 🎮 Controls

### Navigation
- **Previous/Next Buttons** - Move between stages
- **Journey Map** - Click any stage to jump instantly
- **Warp Speed Slider** - Control travel speed (1x - 10x)

### Features
- **📚 Show Facts** - Click to reveal educational information
- **🔊 Audio Toggle** - Enable/disable ambient sounds (top right)
- **📊 Stats** - Monitor distance, time, and discoveries (top right)

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### 3D Planets Not Showing?
- Enable hardware acceleration in browser
- Check WebGL support: visit `webglreport.com`
- Update graphics drivers

### Low FPS?
- Close other tabs
- Reduce warp speed
- Try on a different device

### Achievements Not Unlocking?
- Make sure you reach each stage
- Wait 1-2 seconds at each location
- Check for console errors (F12)

## 📊 Stats to Watch

### Distance
Measured in **AU** (Astronomical Units)
- 1 AU = 150 million km
- Earth to Sun = 1 AU
- Pluto = ~40 AU

### Time
Your journey time in real-time
- Starts when you launch
- Pauses when not playing
- Resets on restart

### Discoveries
Each new stage counts as a discovery
- Max: 10 discoveries
- Track your progress
- Complete them all!
