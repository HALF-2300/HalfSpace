🚀 Space Missions Timeline

From lunar landings to Mars sample return — humanity's journey into the cosmos

2023
🪐

JUICE

ESA

active

🎯 Jupiter moons

Jupiter Icy Moons Explorer studying Ganymede, Callisto, and Europa.

Objectives:

  • Study icy moons
  • Search for subsurface oceans
  • Orbit Ganymede
⚙️

Psyche

NASA

active

🎯 Asteroid Psyche

Mission to explore a unique metal asteroid that may be the core of a protoplanet.

Objectives:

  • Study metallic asteroid
  • Understand planetary cores
  • Map surface composition
2024
🧊

Europa Clipper

NASA

active

🎯 Europa (Jupiter moon)

Detailed study of Jupiter's moon Europa and its subsurface ocean.

Objectives:

  • Map ice shell
  • Analyze ocean composition
  • Identify potential landing sites
2025
🌙

Artemis II

NASA

in development

🎯 Moon (orbit)

First crewed Artemis mission, sending astronauts around the Moon.

Objectives:

  • Test Orion spacecraft with crew
  • Lunar flyby
  • Deep space life support validation
🔭

Vera C. Rubin Observatory

NSF/DOE

in development

🎯 Earth (Chile)

Revolutionary ground telescope that will survey the entire visible sky every few nights.

Objectives:

  • Map dark matter
  • Discover millions of asteroids
  • Detect transient events
🛸

Lunar Gateway

NASA/ESA/JAXA/CSA

in development

🎯 Moon (orbit)

International space station in lunar orbit, supporting Moon and deep space exploration.

Objectives:

  • Enable sustainable lunar presence
  • Deep space research
  • Mars mission staging
2026
👨‍🚀

Artemis III

NASA

planned

🎯 Moon (surface)

First crewed lunar landing since Apollo 17 in 1972.

Objectives:

  • Land humans on Moon
  • South pole exploration
  • Water ice investigation
🔴

Mars Sample Return

NASA/ESA

in development

🎯 Mars

The most ambitious robotic mission ever: returning samples from Mars to Earth.

Objectives:

  • Collect Perseverance samples
  • Launch from Mars surface
  • Earth return by 2033
🚀

Starship Mars Cargo

SpaceX

planned

🎯 Mars

First uncrewed Starship missions to Mars to test landing and deliver supplies.

Objectives:

  • Test Mars entry/landing
  • Deliver cargo
  • Produce propellant from atmosphere
🎨

dearMoon

SpaceX

planned

🎯 Moon (flyby)

First civilian lunar mission, carrying artists and creators around the Moon.

Objectives:

  • Inspire humanity
  • Private lunar tourism
  • Create art from space
2027
🚁

Dragonfly

NASA

in development

🎯 Titan (Saturn moon)

A drone that will fly through Titan's thick atmosphere, exploring this alien world.

Objectives:

  • Study prebiotic chemistry
  • Search for biosignatures
  • Explore diverse terrains
2035
🌍

Habitable Exoplanet Observatory

NASA

planned

🎯 L2 (space)

Next-generation space telescope designed to directly image Earth-like exoplanets.

Objectives:

  • Image habitable zone planets
  • Analyze atmospheres for biosignatures
  • Characterize 30+ exo-Earths
