Venus Day Longer Than Year

A day on Venus (243 Earth days) is longer than its year (225 Earth days). Venus also rotates backwards compared to most planets.

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm larger than Earth that has been raging for at least 400 years. Winds inside this massive storm can reach speeds of up to 432 km/h (268 mph).

Saturn's rings are made mostly of ice particles and rocky debris. They extend up to 282,000 km (175,000 miles) from the planet but are only about 10 meters (33 feet) thick in most places.
